These rules keep it that way.{% endblock %} {% block page_body %}

  • Be respectful

    Everyone is at a different stage. Every question is welcome. Disagree politely.

  • No harassment

    No insults, threats, discrimination, bullying or unwanted private contact.

  • No spam

    No advertising, repeated posts, link-dropping or off-topic promotion.

  • No scholarship scams

    Never ask members for money, "processing fees" or personal documents in exchange for scholarships or admission. Report anyone who does.

  • No impersonation

    Don't pretend to be another person, a university, an official or an App Fee Waiver team member.

  • Don't share false opportunities

    Never intentionally share fake or misleading opportunities. If you notice a mistake, correct it or report it.

  • Provide official sources

    Link to the official university or scholarship page whenever possible.

  • Don't share others' documents

    Never publicly post another person's CV, SOP, letters, transcripts or other personal documents.

  • Respect privacy

    Don't post phone numbers, addresses, ID or passport details, yours or anyone else's. For CV/SOP help, use the private email review.

If something breaks these rules

Use the Report button on the post or comment. Reports are private. Moderators may dismiss a report, remove content, warn the author, or suspend the account.
