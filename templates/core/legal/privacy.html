Last updated: {% now "F j, Y" %}. This is a basic policy structure and should be reviewed by the App Fee Waiver team before public launch.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address and password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Optional profile information: photo, bio, country, institution, field of study, degree level and interests. You choose what to add and which parts are shown publicly.
  • Content you post: posts, comments, reactions, reports and opportunities you submit.
  • Technical information: basic server logs and a session cookie that keeps you logged in.

2. Your email address is private

Your email address is never shown publicly. It is used to log in, to reset your password and to send you account emails.

3. CVs and Statements of Purpose

This website does not accept CV or SOP uploads and does not store them. If you request a review, you send your document by email from your own email account directly to the App Fee Waiver team. That email is handled in the team's inbox, not on this website. Remove anything you don't want to share before sending.

4. Public content

Posts, comments, success stories and submitted opportunities are public. Please avoid posting sensitive personal information publicly, such as phone numbers, home addresses, passport or ID numbers, financial details, or anyone else's personal documents.

5. How we use information

  • To run your account and the community features.
  • To send on-site notifications (for example when someone replies to you).
  • To keep the community safe, including reviewing reports and preventing spam.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Cookies and local storage

We use essential cookies for logging in and for security (CSRF protection). The homepage application checklist is saved only in your own browser.

7. Your choices

  • Edit or remove optional profile details at any time.
  • Edit or delete your own posts and comments.
  • Ask us to delete your account by emailing {{ site.support }}.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy: {{ site.contact }}.
