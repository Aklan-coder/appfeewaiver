About App Fee Waiver

App Fee Waiver is a global student community supporting students with scholarship information, application fee waivers, admissions information and funding opportunities.

Our WhatsApp community is where members share opportunities, ask questions and help each other. This website is the front door: it helps you join the community and showcases funding success stories from our members.

Always confirm deadlines, eligibility and funding details on the official university or scholarship website. App Fee Waiver does not guarantee admission or funding.
