What App Fee Waiver is

App Fee Waiver is a scholarship and university-application community. Members share scholarships, application fee waivers, fully funded programs, assistantships and fellowships; ask and answer questions; and support one another through every stage of the application process.

This website brings the community together in one organised place, alongside our existing groups.

Why the community exists

Applying to universities, especially abroad, can be expensive and confusing. Application fees, scattered information and unclear funding options stop many capable students from applying at all. When students share what they find and what they've learned, everyone applies with better information and lower costs.

Community values

  • Generosity: share what you find and what you've learned.
  • Accuracy: link to official sources and correct mistakes.
  • Respect: everyone is at a different stage, and every question is welcome.
  • Privacy: protect your own and other people's personal information.
  • Integrity: no scams, no impersonation, no false opportunities.

How opportunities are verified

Any member can submit an opportunity. It appears immediately with a Community Submitted label. A moderator then checks that an official source exists (for example the university or scholarship provider's own web page) and, if so, marks it Verified. Listings that look false or misleading are removed.

Verification only means a moderator found an official source. It does not mean App Fee Waiver guarantees admission, funding or eligibility.

Important disclaimer

Always confirm deadlines, eligibility requirements, funding details, and application instructions on the official university or scholarship website before applying. App Fee Waiver is a community resource and is not affiliated with the universities or organisations listed unless stated. Read the full disclaimer.
